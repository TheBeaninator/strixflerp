## What each file does

- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/mmq.cuh`: ggml's MMQ kernel templates with the ROCmFPX load_tiles_* loaders, the mmq_type_traits of the seven types, and the Q7 MMA-direct and panel16 dots.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/mmq.cu`: ggml_cuda_mul_mat_q with the ROCmFPX type cases, the MUL_MAT_ID expert path through mmid.cuh, and the Q7 handoff to the panel16 cache or the Q8 view.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/quantize.cuh`: ggml's q8_1 activation quantizer declarations (block_q8_1_mmq comes from mmq.cuh, which it includes); unchanged base file kept beside mmq.cuh.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/q7-view-mode.cuh`: the Q7 view selector: GGML_ROCM_GFX1151_Q7_PANEL16_CACHE and GGML_ROCM_GFX1151_Q7_Q8_VIEW pick panel16, Q8 view or none.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/q7-panel16.cuh`: the gfx1151 panel16 device layout of canonical Q7 weights (one panel a [16 rows, 256 K] tile, quartets in the mirrored wave32 order).
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/q7-panel16-cache.cuh`: declarations of the process-wide panel16 cache and its MMQ instrumentation (defined in ggml-cuda.cu, not harvested).
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/q7-q8-view.cuh`: the Q7 to standard Q8_0 compute view: sign-extends the stored 7-bit codes and keeps the FP16 group scale, so one Q7 macroblock is eight Q8_0 blocks.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/q7-q8-view-cache.cuh`: declarations of the Q8-view cache (an allowlist of Qwen MMQ projections; GET_ROWS and MMVQ always read canonical Q7; defined in ggml-cuda.cu, not harvested).
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/template-instances/generate_cu_files.py`: the generator of the template-instance files, with the ROCmFPX types in its MMQ and FA-vector lists.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/template-instances/mmq-instance-q2_0_rocmfpx.cu`: DECL_MMQ_CASE(GGML_TYPE_Q2_0_ROCMFPX): the MMQ kernels of that type.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/template-instances/mmq-instance-q3_0_rocmfpx.cu`: DECL_MMQ_CASE(GGML_TYPE_Q3_0_ROCMFPX): the MMQ kernels of that type.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/template-instances/mmq-instance-q4_0_rocmfp4.cu`: DECL_MMQ_CASE(GGML_TYPE_Q4_0_ROCMFP4): the MMQ kernels of that type.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/template-instances/mmq-instance-q4_0_rocmfp4_fast.cu`: DECL_MMQ_CASE(GGML_TYPE_Q4_0_ROCMFP4_FAST): the MMQ kernels of that type.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/template-instances/mmq-instance-q6_0_rocmfpx.cu`: DECL_MMQ_CASE(GGML_TYPE_Q6_0_ROCMFPX): the MMQ kernels of that type.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/template-instances/mmq-instance-q7_0_rocmfpx.cu`: DECL_MMQ_CASE(GGML_TYPE_Q7_0_ROCMFPX): the MMQ kernels of that type.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/template-instances/mmq-instance-q8_0_rocmfpx.cu`: DECL_MMQ_CASE(GGML_TYPE_Q8_0_ROCMFPX): the MMQ kernels of that type.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/mmvq.cu`: ggml's mul_mat_vec_q with the ROCmFPX cases in the vec-dot, VDR and rows-per-block tables and the MUL_MAT_ID expert path (GGML_ROCMFPX_MOE_MMVQ_ROWS_PER_BLOCK and friends are build-time tuning defines).
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/convert.cu`: ggml's to-f16 / to-f32 dequantize kernels with the ROCmFPX cases.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/getrows.cu`: ggml's GET_ROWS with the ROCmFPX dequantizers.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/set-rows.cu`: ggml's SET_ROWS with the ROCmFPX quantizers (the store of a quantized KV cache).
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/cpy.cu`: ggml's CPY with f32 / f16 to ROCmFPX quantization and back.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/cpy-utils.cuh`: device quantizers of the ROCmFPX types for CPY and SET_ROWS (the scale search on the GPU).
- `ggml/rocmfp4/rocmfp4_hip.cu`: standalone ROCmFP4 dequantize kernel for integration tests (rocmfp4_dequantize_q4_0_f32_kernel), linked into ggml-hip.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/fattn-vec.cuh`: ggml's flash_attn_ext_vec with the ROCmFPX K / V cases.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/fattn-common.cuh`: K-dot and V-dequantize helpers of the flash-attention kernels with the ROCmFPX types.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/template-instances/fattn-vec-instance-q3_0_rocmfpx-q3_0_rocmfpx.cu`: DECL_FATTN_VEC_CASE for K = V = Q3_0_ROCMFPX.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/template-instances/fattn-vec-instance-q4_0_rocmfp4-q4_0_rocmfp4.cu`: DECL_FATTN_VEC_CASE for K = V = Q4_0_ROCMFP4.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/template-instances/fattn-vec-instance-q4_0_rocmfp4_fast-q4_0_rocmfp4_fast.cu`: DECL_FATTN_VEC_CASE for K = V = Q4_0_ROCMFP4_FAST.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/template-instances/fattn-vec-instance-q6_0_rocmfpx-q6_0_rocmfpx.cu`: DECL_FATTN_VEC_CASE for K = V = Q6_0_ROCMFPX.
- `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/template-instances/fattn-vec-instance-q8_0_rocmfpx-q8_0_rocmfpx.cu`: DECL_FATTN_VEC_CASE for K = V = Q8_0_ROCMFPX.
