## What each module is

- `kernel.enginelab.core_tiled_wmma`: the shared tiled prefill core adopted from strix-trellis (namespace strix::core): the register-blocked, double-buffered WMMA GEMM for gfx1151 (wmma_tiled.hip: a block owns BM x BN outputs, decodes each weight tile once into LDS, every wave reuses its A / B fragments; v_wmma_f32_16x16x16_f16 with ascending K16 order from +0), the FP16 tile GEMM and its small-M form, the A4 tiles, the v14 core policy, the VGPR budget and wave-uniform helpers, and src/core/NOTICE.md. Why: this is the WMMA prefill engine every model of the lab shares at 8-32K prompts: the decomposition changes but the per-output K16 order does not, so it keeps the EXACT bits of the plain WMMA core; strixflerp's gemm.wmma-tile providers are the slot it would fill; where it would go: beside the catalogue's gemm.wmma-tile/trellis+iu4+f16/f16-f32 providers as the enginelab variant (same op, the engine lab's own operand layouts, so an adapter comes first).
- `kernel.enginelab.core_trellis`: the one EXL3 trellis weight codec of the engine (trellis_format.hpp, trellis_codec.hpp: ring, codebook and reader primitives from strix-trellis, gathered without changing their arithmetic) and the EXL3 row plan. Why: DeepSeek V4.1, GLM and MiniMax keep K4 / K5 trellis weights; this is the decode the dense and MoE trellis kernels inline, next to catalog/strix_trellis's own copy; where it would go: beside the catalogue's linear.decode/exl3-trellis/f16-f32 providers as the enginelab variant (same op, the engine lab's own operand layouts, so an adapter comes first).
- `kernel.enginelab.core_iu4`: stored INT4 (IU4) support: the IU4 pack builder and plans, IU4 x A4 dense projections with their layout and rotation, IU4 row plans and x-pairs, on-the-fly IU4, the INT4 shadow and the small-row W4A16 decoder with streaming stages and shared dequantisation (adopted from strix-trellis, extended here). Why: FAST_I4 is the lab's lossy 4-bit identity for GLM / MiniMax / DS41 dense weights; the W4A16 streaming decoder is the decode GEMV shape for those packs; where it would go: beside the catalogue's linear.decode/iu4/f16-f32 providers as the enginelab variant (same op, the engine lab's own operand layouts, so an adapter comes first).
- `kernel.enginelab.core_integer`: the integer operand for DeepSeek V4.1 GGUF blocks (integer_operand.hpp, integer_math.hpp, the plain / tall / wide / grouped integer main loops and the grouped schedule) and the MoE INT4 arithmetic and chain plans. Why: an exact integer-dot path over GGUF k-quant blocks shared by the dense and the grouped MoE kernels; the lab's local extension of the adopted core; where it would go: beside the catalogue's linear.decode/gguf-kquant/a8-f32 providers as the enginelab variant (same op, the engine lab's own operand layouts, so an adapter comes first).
- `kernel.enginelab.core_bf16`: BF16 kernels of the shared core: dense row plans and narrow plans, the BF16 LM head, the BF16 projection batch, the BF16 reference, the FMA BF16 rows and the tall BF16 plan and reference. Why: routers, hyper-connection projections and heads stay BF16 in every model; these are the shared BF16 GEMV / tall forms with host references for the EXACT gate; where it would go: beside the catalogue's linear.dense/bf16/bf16-f32 providers as the enginelab variant (same op, the engine lab's own operand layouts, so an adapter comes first).
- `kernel.enginelab.core_f16_dense`: FP16 dense forms of the shared core: tall and hi-lo FP16 dense, Q8 x A8 dense, the hi-lo math, the dense linear and row / MMA-group / pair-map / f32-input-major plans and the dense model configuration. Why: the dense projection family every engine binds for its non-expert linears, with the plans that pick tile and split per shape; where it would go: beside the catalogue's linear.dense/f16+q8_0/f16-f32 providers as the enginelab variant (same op, the engine lab's own operand layouts, so an adapter comes first).
- `kernel.enginelab.core_gemv_rows`: the decode GEMV rows family: the GEMV policy and its direct, look-ahead and staged row main loops, row layouts and transforms, resident Q8 rows and row plans, step rows / inputs plans, Q8_0 transpose, low-bit and microscaled FP16 staging, the linear plan table and references, direct row fetch and layer read-ahead. Why: one kernel family for 1-8 row decode projections across weight formats, with the staging that hides UMA reads; the shape strixflerp's linear.decode providers fill; where it would go: beside the catalogue's linear.decode/q8_0+lowbit+microscaled/f16-f32 providers as the enginelab variant (same op, the engine lab's own operand layouts, so an adapter comes first).
- `kernel.enginelab.core_tall`: tall (many-row) linear plans: the tall chunk policy, tall family selection and the tall K plan. Why: the prefill-side split of the same projections when M grows past the rows family; where it would go: beside the catalogue's linear.prefill/-/f16-f32 providers as the enginelab variant (same op, the engine lab's own operand layouts, so an adapter comes first).
- `kernel.enginelab.core_hc`: the hyper-connection core: HC geometry, expand rows, lane chains, the residual mix and its plan, reference and HIP transition, controller mix and controller RMS rows, the mix catalog and latency plan. Why: GLM-5.3 and DeepSeek V4.1 carry multi-stream hyper-connections (mHC); this is the one shared HC family (hc_pre / hc_post chains) the engines bind; where it would go: beside the catalogue's hc.mix/-/f32 providers as the enginelab variant (same op, the engine lab's own operand layouts, so an adapter comes first).
- `kernel.enginelab.core_head`: LM head and output kernels of the shared core: head argmax plans and emit, the screened head (a cheap screen then the exact rows that can win), the Markov head device / reference / round, BF16 embedding rows and the device token-sampling body. Why: the greedy head is a large decode cost; the screened head keeps the exact argmax while reading fewer vocabulary rows; where it would go: beside the catalogue's logits.head/bf16/f32 providers as the enginelab variant (same op, the engine lab's own operand layouts, so an adapter comes first).
- `kernel.enginelab.core_short_conv`: the short causal convolution and its ring buffer state: 2 origin files under src/core. Why: the depthwise conv step of recurrent (GDN / Mamba-style) layers, kept in the shared core; where it would go: a new strixflerp interface mixer.short-conv/-/f32.enginelab, which no catalogue module provides yet.
- `kernel.enginelab.core_tp_join`: the exact tensor-parallel join: tp_join.hpp and tp_join_sum.hpp sum the two ranks' partial outputs in a declared order so a TP2 result is bitwise the single-rank one. Why: the arithmetic half of the lab's exact TP2 decode: partials are joined in the same order the single-node kernel would have accumulated them; where it would go: a new strixflerp interface collective.tp-join/-/f32.enginelab, which no catalogue module provides yet.
- `kernel.enginelab.core_hadamard`: the Hadamard transform helper of the shared core: 1 origin files under src/core. Why: rotations ahead of low-bit operands (IU4 x A4); where it would go: beside the catalogue's rotate.fwht/-/f16 providers as the enginelab variant (same op, the engine lab's own operand layouts, so an adapter comes first).
- `kernel.enginelab.core_activation_quant`: the shared activation quantiser (activation_quant.hpp): 1 origin files under src/core. Why: A8 / A4 activation forms for the integer and IU4 kernels; where it would go: beside the catalogue's quant.activation/-/a8+a4 providers as the enginelab variant (same op, the engine lab's own operand layouts, so an adapter comes first).
- `kernel.enginelab.core_lookup`: the lookup catalog and lookup plan of the shared core: 2 origin files under src/core. Why: table-driven selection of kernel variants by shape; where it would go: a new strixflerp interface kernel-select.lookup/-/-, which no catalogue module provides yet.
- `kernel.enginelab.core_common`: the remaining shared-core headers: backend selection (backend.hpp, backend_hip.hpp), byte budget and range, GLU pair, glu / pointwise / round-store, ordered reduce, numeric partition, NVFP4 BF16 table, null perturbation, block-draft reference, GPU port and SHA-256, weight archive and certificate, weight decode, file source, load queue and slice loader, host lane and free memory, layer set, load cohort, looped layers, window state buffers, HIP host ready, range weight loader and weight uploader. Why: the plumbing the shared kernels and loaders compile against; every other core module includes some of it; where it would go: beside the catalogue's kernel-abi.enginelab-core/-/- providers as the enginelab variant (same op, the engine lab's own operand layouts, so an adapter comes first).
- `kernel.enginelab.attn_core_flash`: the shared dense flash attention: dense decode and its flash form with key split and budget, dense and banded flash prefill, the flash core, tile, prefill policy and tile stage, WMMA fragments and types, online softmax, masks, relative bias, position split and the two-term cost model. Why: one attention family for every engine's dense GQA layers, decode and prefill, WMMA on gfx1151; where it would go: beside the catalogue's attn.softmax-gqa/-/bf16-f32 providers as the enginelab variant (same op, the engine lab's own operand layouts, so an adapter comes first).
- `kernel.enginelab.attn_core_sparse_mla`: selected-token (sparse) attention: the sparse flash MLA, row-group and WMMA forms with key split, the exact selected MLA with its rule and VOPD score body, eager selected attention and the selected block plan and flash prefill. Why: DeepSeek V4.1 and GLM-5.3 attend over a selected token set (DSA / MLA); this is the shared sparse half with an exact rule next to each fast form; where it would go: a new strixflerp interface attn.sparse-mla/-/bf16-f32.enginelab, which no catalogue module provides yet.
- `kernel.enginelab.attn_core_select`: top-k selection for sparse attention: select, radix-3 select and threshold select with their rules and spec, the group select kernel, group score scan and pool, index score (band, mode, WMMA) and the eager softmax rule. Why: the indexer half of DSA-style attention: score the cache, pick the top tokens exactly; where it would go: beside the catalogue's attn.indexer-topk/-/f32 providers as the enginelab variant (same op, the engine lab's own operand layouts, so an adapter comes first).
- `kernel.enginelab.attn_core_prepared`: prepared attention: prepared GQA and BF16 attention with lane plans and prepared step, chunk decode and chunk exact combine (rule), decode and row-group plans, cache rows and shadow, recurrent attention state, and the block / causal GQA references. Why: the plan-and-bind layer the engines call into, with exact chunk combine so a split decode keeps the single-pass bits; where it would go: beside the catalogue's attn.softmax-gqa/-/bf16-f32 providers as the enginelab variant (same op, the engine lab's own operand layouts, so an adapter comes first).
- `kernel.enginelab.attn_contracts`: attention contracts and host references: the MLA contract and reference, the group indexer and its reference, group pool, FP8 plan, cache rows / shadow and prepared lanes (include/strix/attn).
